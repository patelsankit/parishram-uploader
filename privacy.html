Privacy Policy – Parishram Uploader

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Parishram Uploader is a private tool used only by Parishram Farm (YouTube channel “Parishram Farm”) to upload our own farm videos to our YouTube channel.

What data the app accesses

  • The app signs in with Google to the Parishram Farm YouTube account only, using the YouTube Data API.
  • It uses this access only to upload videos and to set their title, description, tags and thumbnail on our own channel.

What we store

  • A sign-in token is stored privately on our own computer so that uploads can run without signing in each time.
  • We do not collect, sell, share or store any personal data of other users. The app is not offered to the public.

Third parties

  • Videos and their details are sent only to YouTube (Google). The app’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can remove the app’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
